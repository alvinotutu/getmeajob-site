GetMeAJob

GetMeAJob is a job-search assistant for people in the UK, used through Telegram. It is a small, private pilot, open only to people invited to try it.

It helps you:

  • find jobs that fit your experience, sent each morning in Telegram (and by email if you choose);
  • write a CV and cover letter for a job you pick, from facts you have given it;
  • keep track of the jobs you apply for, with reminders to follow up;
  • if you choose to connect Gmail, notice replies from employers about your applications.

Read the privacy policy to see what GetMeAJob stores, why, and how to remove it.
