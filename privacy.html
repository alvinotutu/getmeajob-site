Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

GetMeAJob is a job-search assistant used through Telegram, run as a small private pilot by one person in the UK. That person decides how your information is used (the "controller" under UK data protection law). You can reach them at support@getmeajob.vhanp.com.

What GetMeAJob stores

  • Your Telegram ID, so the bot can recognise you.
  • Your profile: the work history, education, skills and other facts you give it, your CV if you send one, and, if you want them on your CV, your name, email, phone number and town.
  • Your job preferences: roles, places, how far you would travel, pay, and employers you have asked it to skip. If you save them, also your notice period (asked when you set up, or in /answers) and your salary expectation (only in /answers), in your own words, for questions application forms ask. You can change or remove them in /answers.
  • Jobs and applications: jobs it has suggested, the ones you picked or turned down, the CVs and letters it wrote for you, and the applications you are tracking with their dates and notes. Each job you track has one record (see Your job records).
  • Email settings: if you turn on email, your address and which messages you want by email.
  • If you connect Gmail: short notes about messages that concern your job applications (see below). Never your emails themselves.
  • Chat with the assistant: we keep your last day of chat messages so the assistant can follow the conversation, then delete them. Notes you ask it to remember stay until you remove them in /memory. We never save notes about health, immigration, money worries or other sensitive topics. If you practise an interview, a short record of how it went is kept.
  • A review log, only if you agree: during the pilot, the bot may ask whether it can keep a copy of your chats with the assistant, to find where it went wrong. You choose in the bot: yes and share, yes without sharing, or no. Saying no changes nothing else. If you say yes, for 30 days it keeps what you typed or said, exactly as you wrote it, the buttons you tapped, its replies and a short note of what the assistant looked up. It never keeps a message that brought up the support reply, voice recordings, the contents of files you sent, Gmail connection codes or payment details. The person who runs GetMeAJob reads it, on the server. Only if you also choose "Yes, and share": about once a week a sample is put through automatic removal of names, employers, places, schools and contact details (which can miss things), and Claude, an AI from Anthropic, reads it to write invented test cases, under Anthropic's business terms (Anthropic is a US company and processes it in the United States, under the standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum). Copies: our storage deletes it after 14 days, the owner's computer when the test cases are written, and Anthropic as described under Who else handles your information (normally within 30 days, longer for content it flags). You can turn either choice off at any time in /privacy; turning the review log off deletes what was kept straight away. A sample already shared can't be recalled, only deleted as above.
  • If you use the browser extension: for each browser you connect, when it was connected, when it was last used and when the connection ends, plus an encrypted key that lets that browser prove it is yours. These are deleted 30 days after the connection ends (see The browser extension).
  • Your feedback: the words you send with /feedback (or in a short, one-line typed message starting "feedback:"), in your own words up to 2,000 characters, and your 👍 or 👎 on a CV, a letter, a day's jobs, a briefing or a form the browser extension filled, with which one it was about. The words are read only by the GetMeAJob team, to make the service better, and are never sent to an AI. A 👍 or 👎 in the bot is a button tap, so if you agreed to the review log it is kept there too, like any other tap (one in the extension isn't). Feedback is kept until you use /delete, and is included in /export.
  • Your credits: your credit balance and a history of what used or added credits (for example "CV and cover letter"), so you can see it with /credits.
  • If you ask to join: when someone not yet using GetMeAJob messages the bot, it keeps their Telegram ID, first name and username so the team can let them in. The name and username are removed as soon as the request is answered; the Telegram ID is kept so the answer stands.

Why, and on what basis

Everything above is used only to give you the service you asked for: finding jobs, writing your documents, tracking applications and reminding you, and, for your feedback, to make that service better. GetMeAJob relies on your consent, which it asks for before it stores anything, and which you can withdraw at any time with /privacy in the bot.

It never sells your information, never uses it for advertising, and never shares it with employers. It sends nothing to an employer for you unless you tap Send on an application or follow-up email it has shown you (see Connecting Gmail), or press Fill in this form in the browser extension, which puts your answers into that employer's form for you to check (see The browser extension). Only you press the site's Submit.

Connecting Gmail (optional)

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, GetMeAJob asks Google for permission to read your mail and to send replies. It uses that access only to:

  • look for messages about your job applications, such as replies from employers or job sites;
  • note what they say about an application (for example, "interview invitation from Leeds Hospital, Tuesday 10am") so it can update your tracker and remind you;
  • help you reply, by writing a draft in the bot that you can read and change first;
  • when an advert asks for applications by email, send your application (your CV and cover letter) or a follow-up to that address, only after you tap Send on the exact email shown.

If your monthly AI allowance is used up, GetMeAJob still checks new mail, but only by its subject line, and sends nothing to Anthropic. When the subject mentions an interview, assessment, offer, shortlisting or an unsuccessful application, it keeps the sender's name, that kind and a fixed sentence (never the subject or the email) and tells you to check Gmail. Once the allowance is back, it reads properly the emails it set aside in the last 35 days, and those it alerted you about in the last 14 days that you haven't answered yet.

The bot can send an email (a reply, or an application or follow-up you ask for) only when you tap Send on the exact text shown. It cannot read your drafts, and it cannot change or delete anything in your mailbox.

It keeps only short notes, linked to your applications. It does not store your emails. The text of an email that might be job-related is sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) only to pick out the job details, then discarded. It is not used to train models, and no person reads it. Mail in your spam folder is checked only for messages from employers and job sites. Other messages are never read or kept beyond checking whether they are job-related.

GetMeAJob's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in the bot, or from your Google Account under Security, then Third-party connections.

The browser extension (optional)

"GetMeAJob: Help me apply" is a Chrome extension that fills in a job application form on any site from the answers you have already approved in the bot. You connect it once by typing a code from /extension. Filling in a form uses no AI. Only if you press Draft answers (see below) does anything go to Anthropic.

  • What it reads: only when you click Fill in this form, and only on that tab: the form's questions (labels, field names, options and nearby headings), whether a field already has something in it (so it never overwrites you), and the page's web address, to match it to your job. The address stays in your browser. It does not read your browsing history or other tabs, and it never reads passwords.
  • What it sends to GetMeAJob: the connection code, once; which of your jobs you are applying for; how many fields it filled in; and when you tell it you have submitted. Each request also carries the browser's connection number, the time and a signature, so we know it is your browser. With the number filled in, it also sends a few counts: the name of the application system the form is on (such as Greenhouse or Workday, worked out in your browser from the address, which itself is not sent), how many fields it saw, filled in and left for you, whether it stopped (for example on a page asking for bank details), and the extension's version. We store them without your account or your job, and with only the week they're from, never the time, and use them only to see where the extension works well. You can email support@getmeajob.vhanp.com to ask about them. With very few users, a week with a single fill could still be matched to that week's credit use, so we treat them as your information too. It never sends what is on the page or what you type into the form. Your internet address reaches our server; it is used only in memory, for up to an hour, to slow down abuse, and is not logged.
  • What it gets from GetMeAJob: the list of your jobs that are ready to apply for (title, employer and the job site's address), and for the job you choose: your approved answers (name, email, phone, town, LinkedIn, right to work and visa sponsorship), your supporting statement and why you want the job, and the CV and cover letter written for it. If you have saved them, it also gets your notice period and salary expectation. These are encrypted so that only your connected browser can read them.
  • Your saved answers (optional): if you have saved a notice period or salary expectation, the extension's window shows each one, with the question it matches, and a Use button. It is put into the form only when you tap Use; nothing is guessed.
  • What happens on the employer's site: the extension puts your answers and files into that site's form. The site can see them as soon as they are filled in, and some sites upload a CV as soon as it is attached, even before you press Submit. Only use it on an application you mean to make.
  • What it keeps in your browser: only the connection key and its end date. Your answers and files are used for that form and not stored by the extension. The Copy buttons put your statement on your clipboard only when you press them.
  • Draft answers (optional): after a fill, you can press Draft answers for the open questions the form still has, such as "Why do you want this role?". Only then, the text of those questions, the job advert and the same facts from your profile that GetMeAJob uses for your cover letter are sent to Anthropic to write drafts. Your name and contact details, your right to work and anything you typed into the form are never sent. Questions about pay, dates, health, convictions, equality monitoring, nationality, referees and similar are never sent: you answer those yourself. Each draft is checked against your profile and shown to you first. It goes into the form only if you press Use these. Anthropic's handling is described under Who else handles your information.
  • Fill the rest with AI (optional, once it is available to you): after a fill, you can press Fill the rest with AI. Only then, the form's visible questions that are still unanswered (not choice buttons, date boxes or file uploads) are sent to GetMeAJob: each one's wording and options, which part of the form it is in, whether it must be answered, and whether it seems to be about someone else; with them, how many job and education rows the form shows and whether it has an "Add another" button for them. Nothing you typed, no other text on the page and not its web address are sent. GetMeAJob sends those questions, the job advert and the facts from your profile behind your CV and cover letter to Anthropic, to choose answers. Your contact details and right to work are never sent: the AI only points to, say, "your email", and GetMeAJob puts in the stored value itself. Questions about pay, notice, health, convictions, equality monitoring, right to work, declarations and similar are never answered for you, and no box is ticked for you except "I currently work here", from your profile. Written answers are checked against your profile. You see every answer first, and nothing goes into the form until you press Use these. It is included in the job's credits.
  • What it never does: press Submit, tick a box for you (except "I currently work here" with Fill the rest with AI, after you press Use these), pay a fee, or send anything anywhere except GetMeAJob and the form you asked it to fill in.

Filling in a form uses credits (see /credits); if nothing was filled in, they are given back. Disconnect in the extension ends that browser's connection; /extension off in the bot ends every browser's. A connection also ends by itself after 90 days, and connecting a fourth browser ends the oldest. On our server we keep, for each browser, when it was connected and last used, when the connection ends, and an encrypted key. A browser's record is deleted 30 days after its connection ends, and a connection code a day after it expires; /delete removes them straight away. The extension's use of your information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements: it is used only to fill in the form you asked for, never sold, never used for advertising, and never read by a person.

Signing in on the website (optional)

You can choose to sign in to GetMeAJob on its website with your email address, as well as using the bot. It is off until you turn it on: send /web in the bot and tap Yes. Only the address you confirmed with /email can be used, and we email that address to say it can now sign you in. Anyone who can read that inbox could then sign in as you, so only turn it on for an inbox that is yours alone.

  • How signing in works: you type your address on the sign-in page and we email you a link. It works once, for 15 minutes. The page always gives the same answer, so it never shows whether an address uses GetMeAJob. An address we don't know gets no email and is never stored; like your internet address, a typed address is held only in memory, for up to an hour, to slow down abuse.
  • The cookie: signing in sets one cookie, __Host-gmj, on api.getmeajob.vhanp.com (where the sign-in page is), which keeps you signed in for 30 days from when you signed in. It is strictly necessary for signing in, so it needs no consent. It is not used for tracking or advertising, and there are no other cookies.
  • What we keep: the address you turned on; for each sign-in link, a scrambled copy (never the link itself) and when it was made, used and expires; and for each signed-in browser, a scrambled copy of its cookie and when it was created, last used, expires and ended. Your internet address is used only in memory, for up to an hour, to slow down abuse, and is not logged.
  • Chatting on the website: the website chat is the same assistant as the bot, with the same choices and limits. While a browser is signed in, we keep a copy of what the bot sends you (replies, buttons, and documents such as your CV and cover letter PDFs) so the website can show it, even if you don't open the page. These copies are deleted once they're a day old (the clean-up runs nightly, so it can take up to two days), when you send /web off, or with /delete. Nobody reads them.
  • How long: the sign-in address is kept until you turn sign-in off or send /delete, and for at most a day after your confirmed email changes. A sign-in link's record is deleted a day after it expires, and a signed-in browser's record 30 days after it is signed out or expires. /delete removes them straight away.
  • Turning it off: Sign out on the website ends that browser's sign-in. /web off in the bot, or changing your email with /email, turns website sign-in off and signs you out everywhere.

Like the rest of GetMeAJob, this relies on your consent: you turn it on with /web, and /web off withdraws it. The link and sign-in records exist only to keep your account secure.

Who else handles your information

  • Amazon Web Services (London, UK) hosts the database, backups and email sending.
  • Anthropic PBC (USA), the AI company whose model GetMeAJob uses, receives the text needed for each task (for example your CV and a job advert, to write your cover letter). Anthropic does not use this to train its models, and for Gmail it receives only the text of a possibly job-related email, only to pick out the job details. Anthropic deletes the text it receives within 30 days. If it is flagged under Anthropic's Usage Policy, it may be kept for up to 2 years, and the scores from its safety checks for up to 7 years. Chat can include sensitive or distressing wording, so we state both periods.
  • OpenAI (USA), only if you allow voice notes: your voice note is sent to OpenAI to be turned into text, with the names of the jobs and employers you're tracking to help it hear them. GetMeAJob doesn't keep the audio. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days for safety checks and doesn't use it for training.
  • Telegram carries your messages with the bot.
  • Google, only if you connect Gmail.
  • Job sites (such as Reed, Adzuna, NHS Jobs, Jooble and Google's job listings) receive the job titles and places being searched for, never your name or contact details.

Who processes your data outside the UK

Your data is stored on Amazon Web Services in London. To read your CV, write your documents and chat with you, the text involved is sent to Anthropic PBC, an AI company in the United States, and processed there. Anthropic deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days, except that content flagged under its usage policy may be kept for up to two years and safety classification scores for up to seven years. The transfer is made under the UK Addendum to the Standard Contractual Clauses in Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum, which is part of its commercial terms of service.

If you allow voice notes, the audio and the names of the jobs and employers you are tracking are sent to OpenAI, also a US company, which may keep the audio for up to 30 days for safety checks and does not use it for training; GetMeAJob never keeps the audio. You can refuse AI use, voice notes or Gmail separately in /privacy at any time.

Your job records

For every job you track, GetMeAJob keeps one record: the advert and what it asked for, the CV and cover letter written for it, the dates and status of your application, interview time and place, a short note of what each relevant email said (never the email itself), your interview practice results, and how it ended. This record is kept until the job is over plus six months. A job that has not changed for six months is treated as over, so an open job's record is deleted twelve months after its last change. Each job shows the date it will be forgotten, and a nightly process deletes records when that date comes.

In /applications you can tap Full record to see everything held about a job, Send me a copy to receive it as files, or Forget this job to delete that one job now; /export and /delete still cover everything at once. Your chat messages are separate and are deleted after one day. Backups are kept for 35 days and older copies for 7 more, so a deleted record can remain in a backup for up to 42 days before it is gone everywhere. The legal basis is your agreement when you start, which you can withdraw at any time in /privacy.

How long it is kept

Your information is kept while you use GetMeAJob, and each job's record only as long as Your job records says. When you send /delete, it is removed from the live database straight away, and from backups within 42 days. Sent emails are removed from the email queue after 30 days. Your credit history is deleted with the rest of your information, except that a record of each payment you made (amount, date and Telegram's payment reference, without your name) is kept for 6 years for accounting, even after /delete. A review log entry is deleted after 30 days (and from backups within 42 days more), or straight away when you turn the review log off in /privacy, say no when asked, or send /delete, and /export includes it. A request to join that nobody answers is closed after 14 days. The extension's weekly fill counts are kept for 12 months; because they are stored without your account, /delete and /export can't find them, but you can email us to ask about them.

Your rights

In the bot, /export sends you everything GetMeAJob holds about you with your account (for the extension, the list of connected browsers, never their keys; for website sign-in, your sign-in address and the dates of each sign-in, never the cookie), /delete removes it all (except those payment records, and the extension's weekly fill counts, which are stored without your account and kept 12 months), and /privacy lets you change your choices. You can also email support@getmeajob.vhanp.com to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion, or with any question. If you are unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the bot will tell you before the change applies.
