Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

GetMeAJob is a job-search assistant used through Telegram, run as a small private pilot by one person in the UK. That person decides how your information is used (the "controller" under UK data protection law). You can reach them at a.c.otutu93@gmail.com.

What GetMeAJob stores

  • Your Telegram ID, so the bot can recognise you.
  • Your profile: the work history, education, skills and other facts you give it, your CV if you send one, and, if you want them on your CV, your name, email, phone number and town.
  • Your job preferences: roles, places, how far you would travel, pay, and employers you have asked it to skip.
  • Jobs and applications: jobs it has suggested, the ones you picked or turned down, the CVs and letters it wrote for you, and the applications you are tracking with their dates and notes.
  • Email settings: if you turn on email, your address and which messages you want by email.
  • If you connect Gmail: short notes about messages that concern your job applications (see below). Never your emails themselves.
  • Your credits: your credit balance and a history of what used or added credits (for example "CV and cover letter"), so you can see it with /credits.
  • If you ask to join: when someone not yet using GetMeAJob messages the bot, it keeps their Telegram ID, first name and username so the team can let them in. The name and username are removed as soon as the request is answered; the Telegram ID is kept so the answer stands.

Why, and on what basis

Everything above is used only to give you the service you asked for: finding jobs, writing your documents, tracking applications and reminding you. GetMeAJob relies on your consent, which it asks for before it stores anything, and which you can withdraw at any time with /privacy in the bot.

It never sells your information, never uses it for advertising, and never shares it with employers. It sends nothing to an employer for you.

Connecting Gmail (optional)

If you choose to connect your Gmail account, GetMeAJob asks Google for permission to read your mail and to send replies. It uses that access only to:

  • look for messages about your job applications, such as replies from employers or job sites;
  • note what they say about an application (for example, "interview invitation from Leeds Hospital, Tuesday 10am") so it can update your tracker and remind you;
  • help you reply, by writing a draft in the bot that you can read and change first.

The bot can send a reply only when you tap Send on the exact text shown. It cannot read your drafts, and it cannot change or delete anything in your mailbox.

It keeps only short notes, linked to your applications. It does not store your emails. The text of an email that might be job-related is sent to an AI provider (Anthropic) only to pick out the job details, then discarded. It is not used to train models, and no person reads it. Mail in your spam folder is checked only for messages from employers and job sites. Other messages are never read or kept beyond checking whether they are job-related.

GetMeAJob's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time in the bot, or from your Google Account under Security, then Third-party connections.

Who else handles your information

  • Amazon Web Services (London, UK) hosts the database, backups and email sending.
  • Anthropic, the AI company whose model GetMeAJob uses, receives the text needed for each task (for example your CV and a job advert, to write your cover letter). Anthropic does not use this to train its models, and for Gmail it receives only the text of a possibly job-related email, only to pick out the job details.
  • OpenAI (USA), only if you allow voice notes: your voice note is sent to OpenAI to be turned into text, with the names of the jobs and employers you're tracking to help it hear them. GetMeAJob doesn't keep the audio. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days for safety checks and doesn't use it for training.
  • Telegram carries your messages with the bot.
  • Google, only if you connect Gmail.
  • Job sites (such as Reed, Adzuna, NHS Jobs, Jooble and Google's job listings) receive the job titles and places being searched for, never your name or contact details.

How long it is kept

Your information is kept while you use GetMeAJob. When you send /delete, it is removed from the live database straight away, and from backups within 35 days. Sent emails are removed from the email queue after 30 days. Your credit history is deleted with the rest of your information. A request to join that nobody answers is closed after 14 days.

Your rights

In the bot, /export sends you everything GetMeAJob holds about you, /delete removes it all, and /privacy lets you change your choices. You can also email a.c.otutu93@gmail.com to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion, or with any question. If you are unhappy with the answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the bot will tell you before the change applies.
